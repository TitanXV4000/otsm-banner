OTSM case banner

A Chrome and Edge extension that keeps the important facts about a case in one line at the top of every OTSM case, with the contact's other cases, the account's entitlements and FTS one click away.

Download version @VERSION@ @DATE@ · Chrome or Edge on Windows, Mac or Linux · Source on GitHub

What it shows

  • Case number (click to copy), Title (shortened; hover for all of it), Priority and Status badges, Owner, Contact, Account, Created and Updated.
  • Product Dev ID when the case has one, linked to its Quixy page. Product and Entitlement can be turned on in the settings.
  • FTS opens the case's FTS account in WinSCP, logged in with the case's credentials.
  • Contact and account cards with phone numbers, the account's support team and special handling notes.
  • It stays on screen as you scroll, and reloads when you press OTSM's Refresh button on the case.

Contact and account cards

Hover over the contact or account name for their details: email and phone numbers, time zone with the local time there, location, account number and region. Click to keep the card open. The name at the top of a card opens the contact's or account's page in OTSM, and phone numbers can call through your phone app or Cisco Jabber.

The account card also shows the support team: the TAM, SAM, premium manager and support group from the case's entitlement (or the account's other entitlements), and the account's special handling notes.

Other cases

The case icon next to the contact lists the contact's other cases, or the whole account's, newest first, with links. Cases the contact follows are included and marked, as they are in the contact's own portal. This case is highlighted and closed cases are greyed.

Entitlements

The shield icon next to the account lists its entitlements: product, support plan, contract, dates and state, in-use ones first. A ✓ marks the ones the case's contact is an entitled user on, and the case's own entitlement is highlighted.

Settings

Click ⚙ on the banner (or right-click the extension's icon and choose Options) to pick which fields show and in what order, the colours, the title length, how names are shown (First Last or Last, First), whether phone numbers open your phone app or Jabber, and which FTS account the button uses. Changes show on open cases straight away.

Install

The extension isn't in the Chrome or Edge store, so you load it from a folder. It takes a minute:

  1. Download the zip above and extract it somewhere you'll keep it, such as Documents\otsm-banner. The browser runs the extension from that folder, so don't delete it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome, or edge://extensions in Edge.
  3. Turn on Developer mode (top right in Chrome, on the left in Edge).
  4. Click Load unpacked and pick the otsm-banner folder (the one with manifest.json in it).
  5. Open or refresh an OTSM case. The banner appears at the top.

Update to a new version

  1. Download the new zip and extract it over the old folder, replacing the files.
  2. On the extensions page, click the reload arrow on the OTSM case banner card, then refresh your OTSM tabs.

FTS in WinSCP

The FTS button needs WinSCP installed. The first time, the browser asks whether to open WinSCP; tick Always allow so it doesn't ask again.

Privacy

The extension only runs on OTSM case pages. It reads the case, contact, account, entitlement and special handling note details from OTSM with your own login, the same way the OTSM page does, and doesn't send anything anywhere else. Your settings are kept in your browser profile.
