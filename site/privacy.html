OTSM case banner: privacy

Last updated @DATE@ (version @VERSION@)

The OTSM case banner is a browser extension for people who work in OpenText Service Manager (OTSM, also called SMAX) at us42-smax.saas.microfocus.com. It is an independent tool. It is not made, endorsed or supported by OpenText or Micro Focus.

What it reads

While you have an OTSM page open, the extension asks OTSM for details of the case you're looking at: the case itself, its contact, account, entitlements, support team, special handling notes and other cases of the same contact or account. It asks with your own OTSM sign-in, so it can only see what you can already see in OTSM.

What it does with it

It shows those details on the OTSM page, in the banner and its cards. They are not saved and are not sent anywhere except back to your screen. The extension has no server of its own, and no analytics, tracking or ads. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

What it changes

Only one thing: when you pick a new status for a case from an OTSM case list, the extension saves that status to OTSM as you, the same way OTSM's own Save button would. You can turn this off in the extension's settings.

What it stores

Only your settings for the extension (which fields to show, colours and similar choices), in your browser's extension storage. If you use your browser's sync, the browser syncs them to your other computers. No case data is stored.

Links it opens

Some parts of the banner open other programs or sites when you click them: phone numbers open Jabber, FTS opens WinSCP or your FTP program, and Product Dev IDs open Quixy. Those programs and sites have their own privacy terms.
